About the project

DiabetesStage

A university machine-learning demonstration built to make model evaluation, privacy choices and limitations visible—not to imitate a medical diagnosis.

Privacy

No account, database or server-side history exists. A prediction request is processed in memory and discarded. The application does not log complete inputs or persist them in browser local storage.
